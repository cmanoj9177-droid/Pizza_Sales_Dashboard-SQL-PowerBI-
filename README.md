Key Insights

Calculated total revenue to measure overall business performance.

Analyzed average order value to understand customer spending behavior.

Identified total pizzas sold and total orders to measure demand.

Found average pizzas per order to understand buying patterns.

Identified top-selling pizzas that drive revenue.

Identified low-performing pizzas that need improvement or removal.
